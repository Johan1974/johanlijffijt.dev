Tumble — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Tumble is a small, independently built app by Johan Lijffijt. This policy explains what data Tumble collects, why, and how it's handled — in plain language, not legal boilerplate.

What we collect

  • Account info: if you create an account, your email address and password (handled by our authentication provider, never stored as plain text).
  • Habit data: the habits you add and the dates you mark them complete, so your progress can sync across your devices.
  • Feedback (optional): if you use the in-app feedback form, the answers you give and, only if you choose to enter it, your email address.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored on a self-hosted Supabase instance running on infrastructure we manage directly — not a third-party data broker. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Why we collect it

Purely to make the app work: syncing your habits across devices, and — if you submit feedback — improving Tumble based on what real testers tell us.

Analytics & advertising

The app itself does not include any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Data retention & deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can request full deletion of your account and data at any time by contacting us (see below).

Children

Tumble does not verify age. If you are under 16, please have a parent or guardian help you create an account and review this policy with you.

Changes

As Tumble develops (for example, if paid subscriptions are added later), this policy will be updated. The current version is always available at this address.

Contact

Questions, or want your data deleted? Use the contact form or the feedback form — we don't publish a direct email address here to keep spam out, but every message reaches us.
